Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about working with WayKON Capital

What is WayKON Capital?

WayKON Capital is an early-stage holding company focused on investing in, acquiring, and building businesses across creative industries including fashion, music, art, advertising, and media.

What industries do you focus on?

We focus on creative industries including fashion, digital infrastructure, music, art, advertising, media, and brand development.

What stage companies do you invest in?

We primarily focus on early-stage companies with proven product-market fit and clear growth potential. We also acquire and build businesses from the ground up.

How do you support portfolio companies?

We provide comprehensive support including strategic advisory, operational guidance, financial planning, market expansion, team building, and access to our network.

Can I work with you without investment?

Yes, we offer advisory and consulting services to creative businesses. Contact us to discuss how we can support your growth without equity investment.

How do I get in touch?

You can reach us via our contact form or email us directly at info@waykoncapital.com. We respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours.
