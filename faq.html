About WayKON Capital

WayKON Capital is an early-stage holding company focused on the creative industries. We partner with visionary founders and creative teams to build enduring businesses.

From fashion brands redefining style to digital platforms amplifying innovation—we invest in the creators and operators driving cultural and commercial change, with a long-term vision for growth across the creative sector.

Based between London and Lagos, our approach combines creative expertise with strategic support to unlock growth and long-term value.

Frequently Asked Questions

General

WayKON Capital is an early-stage holding company focused on investing in, acquiring, and building businesses across creative industries including fashion, music, art, advertising, and media. We provide strategic capital and operational support to help creative businesses scale.

We focus on creative industries including fashion, digital infrastructure, music, art, advertising, media, and brand development. Our portfolio spans companies that blend creativity with commerce.

WayKON Capital operates between London, United Kingdom, and Lagos, Nigeria, allowing us to bridge European and African creative markets.

Investment

We primarily focus on early-stage companies with proven product-market fit and clear growth potential. We also acquire and build businesses from the ground up in strategic sectors.

Our process begins with an initial conversation to understand your business, market, and vision. We then conduct due diligence, develop a strategic plan, and structure a partnership that aligns with your long-term goals. The entire process typically takes 4-8 weeks.

We're flexible depending on the situation. We can take minority stakes in growing businesses or acquire majority/full ownership when building or turning around companies. Our goal is to structure partnerships that work for all stakeholders.

Partnership

We provide comprehensive support including strategic advisory, operational guidance, financial planning, market expansion, team building, and access to our network of partners and resources.
