About WayKON Capital

WayKON Capital is an early-stage holding company focused on the creative industries. We partner with visionary founders and creative teams to build enduring businesses.

From fashion brands redefining style to digital platforms amplifying innovation—we invest in the creators and operators driving cultural and commercial change, with a long-term vision for growth across the creative sector.

Based between London and Lagos, our approach combines creative expertise with strategic support to unlock growth and long-term value.

Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

WayKON Capital is an early-stage holding company focused on the creative industries. Based between London and Lagos, we invest in and build businesses across fashion, digital infrastructure, and brand development, with a long-term vision for growth across the creative sector.

We specialize in creative industries and take a founder-friendly, long-term approach to building businesses. We provide hands-on operational support and understand the unique balance between artistic vision and commercial success.

We focus on early-stage companies in the creative industries, typically with founding teams and clear creative vision. We look for businesses that align with our focus areas in fashion, digital infrastructure, and brand development.

Investment Criteria

We focus on creative industries including fashion, digital infrastructure for brands, brand development, and related cultural sectors. We're particularly interested in businesses at the intersection of creativity and commerce.

We seek exceptional founders with clear vision, cultural relevance, scalable business models, and strong growth potential. We value authentic storytelling, operational excellence, and businesses that can make meaningful cultural impact.

Investment amounts vary based on the company's stage, needs, and growth potential. We take a flexible approach and structure deals that align with founder goals and company trajectory.

Working with WayKON

We're hands-on partners. We provide strategic guidance, operational support, network access, and expertise while respecting founder autonomy. Our involvement is tailored to each company's specific needs.

We provide strategic advisory, operational support, growth strategy, financial management guidance, talent acquisition support, and access to our network of industry connections and resources.

We're long-term partners focused on building sustainable businesses. We're not looking for quick exits—we invest with a multi-year perspective aligned with each company's growth trajectory.
